Qwen cache entries use **explicit** cache pricing, consistent with the prior catalog. The public Beijing model-detail console lists Max/2.4T cache hits at CNY 1 (implicit hits CNY 1.5), and Flash cache writes at CNY 1.25, not the generic 125% of its CNY 0.8 input rate. Prime and specialized multimodal tiers without verified full token/cache pricing are not newly mapped.

GLM prices follow its detailed API price table. Undated marketing text advertising a temporary half-price offer is not enough to establish historical or future promotion boundaries.

## Event-time and historical boundaries

- Gemini Flash introductory pricing ends December 31, 2026. From January 1, 2027, the published prices are USD 1.50 input / 7.50 output / 0.15 cache read. The catalog uses 00:00 UTC because the price table gives a date without an exact instant. Requests with no supplied timestamp use the current date, including active promotions.
- Gemini 3.6's original discount start could not be established. Its old estimates are preserved before **September 29, 2026 00:00 UTC**, the catalog adoption boundary, not a claimed official repricing date. New 3.7/3.8 model IDs correct previously estimated fallback costs.
- DeepSeek V4 Pro's new schedule begins **August 16, 2026 16:00 UTC**; V4 Flash's verified new rates begin **September 10, 2026 04:00 UTC**. Complete old entries are retained before those boundaries, with no new multiplier applied to them. Unverified intermediate Flash pricing remains the prior catalog baseline rather than a guessed retrospective revision.
- DeepSeek peak hours are Monday–Friday **01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC**, excluding Chinese public holidays. All other hours use half the peak rates for every token partition. Holiday dates use Asia/Shanghai and the official 2026 calendar; the calendar needs updating when later years are announced. Saturday/Sunday remain off-peak, including make-up workdays.
- Current DeepSeek documentation still lists V4 Pro separately. It is not remapped to Flash based on an older migration announcement. Legacy `deepseek-chat` / `deepseek-reasoner` entries remain unchanged without a verified current alias contract.
- The catalog fingerprint triggers cost-cache rebuilding. Rebuilding uses usage-event timestamps; it does not apply today's prices to every historical request. Existing cache files and source logs are not deleted. Newly recognized model IDs can change prior fallback estimates.
