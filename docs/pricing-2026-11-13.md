# November 13, 2026 pricing audit

Version 0.2.12 uses the shared API-equivalent catalog for Claude, Codex and
OpenCode. Prices below are USD per million tokens, not subscription bills.

## Changes

| Model | Input | Output | Cache read | Cache write 5m / 1h |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Claude Fable / Mythos 5.2 | 8 | 40 | 0.20 | 10 / 16 |
| Gemini 4 Carbon through November 30 | 2 | 10 | 0.10 | Not supported |
| Gemini 4 Carbon from December 1 | 4 | 20 | 0.20 | Not supported |
| Gemini 4 Hydrogen | 1.50 | 7.50 | 0.075 | Not supported |

Sources: [Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing),
[Fable model ID](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-2/overview),
[Mythos model ID](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/mythos-5-2/overview),
and [Google pricing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing).

Gemini 4 uses implicit caching only. The catalog's legacy write slots retain the
normal input-rate fallback for unclassified local input, not a claim that an
explicit-cache-write product exists. Claude Fast/Batch and Google special tiers
are not newly inferred from logs without tier attribution.

Google also announces that `gemini-2.5-pro` routes to 3.8 Flash on December 1:
its complete previous entry, including >200K context rates, is preserved before
that date. December uses 0.75 / 3.75 / 0.075 (input/output/cache read), then
1.50 / 7.50 / 0.15 from January 1, 2027. Date-only boundaries use 00:00 UTC,
consistent with the existing catalog convention. The 2.5 Flash → 3.5 Flash-Lite
migration has no change to currently mapped text-token rates.

## Provider recheck

- [OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing): mapped general
  GPT-6.x/5.6 rates, long-context prices and supported speed tiers unchanged.
  GPT-5.6 Sol promotion still ends November 30; no unannounced reversion inferred.
- Anthropic: added Fable/Mythos 5.2; 5.1, Opus/Sonnet/Haiku 5.5 prices unchanged.
- Google: additions and dated routing above; existing Argon/Flash promotion
  boundaries and other checked general text rates unchanged.
- [Qwen](https://help.aliyun.com/zh/model-studio/model-pricing): public Mainland
  Qwen3.8 Max/Flash/2.4T/27B rates unchanged (direct, TLS-verified read).
  The checked public table has no Qwen4 price entry; no price is inferred from
  preview model availability in clients. Explicit-cache rates retain their
  September 29 verification date; no authenticated console recheck is claimed.
- [GLM](https://docs.bigmodel.cn/cn/guide/start/pricing): mapped GLM-5.x text and
  cache-hit rates unchanged.
- [DeepSeek](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/): Flash/Pro rates
  and peak/off-peak rules unchanged.

Scope remains the existing coding/text catalog. Specialized legal/cyber,
image/audio/video, tools, storage, account-specific discounts and regional
uplifts are excluded. Older entries absent from current official tables remain
unchanged. The configured USD/CNY rate remains 7; it is not a live FX quote.

## Historical protection

All previous historical entries are retained. Only new model IDs and Gemini
2.5 Pro's announced future routing are added. Historical usage is priced by
event time during the compressed-archive rebuild; rates for existing models
before December 1 remain unchanged. Newly recognized IDs correct former
fallback estimates. Original source files and prior caches remain intact.
